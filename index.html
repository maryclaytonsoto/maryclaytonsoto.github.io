About

I'm a biomedical engineering and mathematics double major at Duke University's Pratt School of Engineering, with a minor in visual arts. I'm drawn to the places these fields overlap — medical device design, patent law, and the intersection of art and science.


Right now, I'm designing surgical education models with Duke's SEAL Lab through Bass Connections, coordinating cardiovascular device research at the Tallahassee Research Institute, teaching Python to first-year engineers as an EGR105 TA, and serving as Vice President of Professional Development for Theta Tau.
