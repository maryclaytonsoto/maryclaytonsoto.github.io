Hero Sketch Options

Four hand-drawn directions on the warm cream background with the muted, earthy palette — each keeps a bright pink→coral→orange ombre as the focal accent, since that's the piece worth keeping. Each sketch sits in a mock hero so you can judge it in context. Tell Claude which letter (or mix) wins and the whole site gets refactored to match.
